# Collector prompt — who talks about the company: affiliates, reviewers, media, creators (sonnet, background)

Placeholders: {domain}, {brand}, {aliases}, {category}.
Your final message IS the deliverable; finish, do not defer. Public data only; no logins / sign-ups; people by role unless they are public publishers or creators (channel / site names are fine). ToolSearch "select:WebFetch,WebSearch" + Ahrefs MCP (`serp-overview`, `site-explorer-referring-domains`, `site-explorer-all-backlinks`; `doc` first). Units → `python costs.py {domain} "Ahrefs API" <units> units "affiliate and media"` in the repo root.

Write `data/{domain}/affiliate_media.json` (source "affiliate_media", contract `schema.json`, url REQUIRED and real, quotes verbatim ≤ 25 words, no em dashes):
1. **Money SERPs**: Ahrefs `serp-overview` (country us) for "{brand} review", "{brand} alternatives", "{brand} pricing", "{brand} vs" + top competitor, "best {category}". For each top-10 result: url, title, position, publisher type = affiliate | review platform | media | competitor | own | UGC / forum | directory, and whether the page carries affiliate links to {domain} (WebFetch the page; look for referral params, partner disclosures, PartnerStack / FirstPromoter / impact links). Items type "page", meta {query, position, publisher_type, affiliate_link: true|false|unknown, mentions_brand_first: bool}.
2. **Affiliate footprint**: count of distinct sites with detected affiliate links to {domain} and their top 10 by Ahrefs DR; the program's public terms (commission, cookie, restrictions) if not already in marketplaces.json. Summary metric.
3. **Media and PR**: referring domains with DR ≥ 60 that are media, analyst or industry publications (last 12 months, from referring-domains sorted by DR), with the linking article title and date. Items type "mention", meta {publisher_type: media|analyst|industry, dr, first_seen}. Plus press releases on wire services and podcast / conference appearances found with WebSearch then WebFetch.
4. **Creators**: YouTube videos reviewing or comparing {brand} by third-party channels (WebSearch "site:youtube.com {brand} review" then fetch the watch page for channel, views, date). Items type "post", meta {platform: youtube, channel, views, date}.
5. **Share of voice in money SERPs**: for "best {category}", which brands appear in the top 10 pages' titles / H2 (count per brand). One metric item, meta {brand_counts}.
SERP features (AI Overview, discussion blocks) are their own publisher type, not 'own'; report counts as 'results', not slots. For partner posts on third-party platforms record who authored them (the company itself or a third party). Media items: open the page; a 404 / deleted page is not coverage; give the article date, not only Ahrefs first-seen. Never invent; mark blocked fetches. Final message: money-SERP publisher mix, affiliate site count, top media mentions, creators, SOV.
