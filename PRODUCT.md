# PRODUCT — Marketing OSINT report pages

## Register
product (a generated report: the design serves reading and decision, not persuasion)

## Users & purpose
A marketer or CMO opens a private link between meetings, on a laptop in a daylit office, and needs in 2 minutes: what this company promises, to whom, through which channels, whether customers agree, and what to do about it. They come back to check evidence behind a claim.

## Brand
Marketing OSINT design system, defined in `DESIGN.md` (tokens in `design/tokens.css`): serif headlines, Inter body, mono citations, near-black ink on white and light-grey surfaces, one teal highlighter used sparingly. Semantic hues only for verdicts.

## Principles
- Answer first: TL;DR and "so what" are reachable without scrolling far.
- Every claim is one click from its source (numbered citations + source list).
- Show uncertainty: confidence per section, gaps listed, never hidden.
- Dense but scannable: tables and rows over cards; no decorative motion.

## Anti-references
SaaS hero-metric dashboards, card grids, gradient accents, eyebrow kickers on every section.
